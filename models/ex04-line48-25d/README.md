# ex04-line48-25d (Example 2 of the paper, 2.5D)

2.5D two-layer earth as ex01 (100 ohm-m, 50 m over 1000 ohm-m); 48 surface electrodes from
-470 to 470 m (20 m spacing) on the y axis; pole-pole rows from both line ends and dipole-dipole rows
with n = 1..8 (426 rows; 47 of the 48 electrodes carry current). Core cells 10 m x 10 m;
outer extents 1000 m: 2360 cells, 2000 m: 2728 cells, 20000 m: 4002 cells. Runs: group example2 of scripts/paper_runs.py.
