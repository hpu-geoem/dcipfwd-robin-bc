# ex03-line48-3d (Example 2 of the paper, 3D)

3D two-layer earth as ex01 (100 ohm-m, 50 m over 1000 ohm-m); 48 surface electrodes from
-470 to 470 m (20 m spacing) on the x axis; pole-pole rows from both line ends and dipole-dipole rows
with n = 1..8 (426 rows; 47 of the 48 electrodes carry current). Core cells 20 m x 20 m x 10 m;
outer extents 1000 m: 25600 cells, 2000 m: 40040 cells, 20000 m: 92568 cells. Runs: group example2 of scripts/paper_runs.py.
